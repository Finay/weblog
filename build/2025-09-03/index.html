A fresh start on day 2

When I decided I was going to write a quick post today, I wasn't aware that it was exactly 1 year ago that I wrote the first proper post on this blog "A fresh start on day 94". In it, I declared quite a lot of goals, most primarily, that I was going to completely change the trajectory of my gap year from that day onwards. Clearly, that didn't happen. In fact, I didn't complete a single concrete goal I set in that post. Though at the same time I'm not dissapointed about that fact. If anything, I'm proud I made that post, glad my year played out the way it did. It definitely could have gone better but thats in the past. Right now, I'm content.

Classes just started yesterday. I'm getting used to Toronto. Life's hectic but I'm managing. I'm worrying, but I'm also moving. And in this moment, I know which one matters between the two.
