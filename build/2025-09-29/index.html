4 Week Tofu

I just started my 5th week of classes at UofT. Can you believe that?? Btw, I like spelling UofT as tofu since I find it rolls a little better and I like tofu. Also, I had my first term test earlier today. CSC110. 5% of my grade. Oddly, no adrenaline. I was out on minute 41 out of 100. Pretty cocky. Maybe too confident.
Life is going beautifully. I spend most days incredibly socially (at least relative to my gap year) and I find myself putting myself out there, whether it be joining intramural sports, getting to know friends of friends, reaching out to hang out with people, or pulling up to random club events. More often than not, I find myself simply studying without a looming deadline. Though that doesn't mean I've been entirely on top of all assignments (some have slipped through the cracks). I think a simple way to put it is that managing my health and time entirely turned out to be more of an adventure than a challenge.
Funnily enough, I'm honestly a little bored right now. I've saturated study to the point I believe I will be more efficient studying next week (like I could review stuff now but absorption would be low or redundant). So I'm left thinking what else I could do. I've been working on a really cool note taking app but then again, its complete in the sense it already achieves it's original purpose and further developments make it nicer but thats an endless hole. I don't see what my next "step" is. Maybe I have to wait. I applied to a bunch of competitive clubs that work on cool projects from AI stuff to NPO stuff. But theres nothing I can do till results come back (unfortunately not looking optimistic) and like, in the case I get rejected, what was I lacking in my application? I've been coding for over 6 years. I know I am capable of roles I apply for. I've attributed the lack of technical opportunities to my lack of putting myself out there but now that I have, if I still get rejected, I'm kinda lost on what I was supposed to do. Will I be ready after 7 years? Is that what it takes?
This thinking is getting me nowhere. I just dumped a bunch of random worries in a poor recap of tofu so far. Maybe I'll write a better post explaining stuff later but I'm out of juice for now.
